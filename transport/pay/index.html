Only the people listed below can open this page or see any cost that includes driver pay. Every time it's opened or changed is logged at the bottom.

Drivers

Monthly cost: employees, charged every weekday whether or not they drive. A full month comes to exactly the monthly cost; a part month is shared by weekdays, so give someone who starts or leaves mid-month a start or end date. Part-time: a set number of days a month paid whatever, at a day rate, plus the day rate for any extra days. Day rate: agency and temporary drivers, charged only for days they drive.
